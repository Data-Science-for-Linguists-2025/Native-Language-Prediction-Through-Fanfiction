# LICENSE
## The Data
The data sourced for this project comes from [Archive of Our Own](archiveofourown.org).  
To read more about content ownership on AO3, click [here](https://archiveofourown.org/tos_faq#define_transformative). In summary,
    A fanwork creator holds the rights to their own content, just the same as any professional author, artist, or other creator.
The data used in this project will **not** be available for download. Distributing any and all datasets containing AO3 fanworks is a violation of their TOS which forbids reposting of fanworks.  
The *methods* of finding the data **will** be available (see: [Nat_Finder.py](natfinder/natfinder/spiders/Nat_Finder.py)). These may be used to collect data, but AO3 *does* rate limit to prevent DDoS attacks and mass collection of data with the purpose to share it.  
To see an example of what a fanfiction may look like, visit [here](https://archiveofourown.org/works/22837771).
